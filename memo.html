Letters from the Sea

During my time in the US Navy, I carried a small green memo notebook where I wrote diary entries and love letters. This digital recreation lets you flip through those pages and experience the intimate thoughts of a sailor far from home.

March 15th, 1995
Dear Sarah,

Another day at sea. The Pacific stretches endlessly in all directions, and I find myself thinking of you constantly. The way you smiled when I left port still haunts my dreams.

Today we had general quarters drill at 0600. The alarm bells always remind me of how far I am from your gentle voice calling my name in the morning.

I miss the simple things - coffee together on Sunday mornings, your laugh when I told terrible jokes, the way you'd steal my Navy sweater because it smelled like me.
March 18th, 1995
The stars are incredible out here, away from all the city lights. Last night I spent my watch thinking about that time we laid on the beach and you pointed out constellations I'd never noticed.

Got your letter today! The mail call always makes my heart race - hearing my name called means another piece of home has found its way across the ocean to me.
March 20th, 1995
Storm today. Big swells, rain coming sideways. The ship rocks like a cradle, but not the gentle kind that soothes you to sleep. Makes me think about solid ground and your steady presence.
March 25th, 1995
Today marks one month since we left port. One month since I've held you, kissed you, heard your voice without static from the radio room.

The guys in my berthing have started calling me "Romeo" because I'm always writing in this little green book. If they only knew these aren't just letters - they're lifelines. Each word connects me back to the life I'm fighting to return to.

I dream of the day I can close this notebook forever, not because I don't want to write to you, but because I'll be able to whisper these words directly in your ear instead.
March 28th, 1995
We're heading toward port for resupply - still weeks away, but the thought of solid ground and a phone call to you keeps me going through these long watches.

I've been thinking about what I want to say when I call. There's so much stored up in my heart, so many moments I've wanted to share with you. The sunset that painted the entire ocean gold. The dolphins that raced alongside our ship. The way the moon creates a silver path on the water that seems to lead straight home to you.
April 2nd, 1995
This may be my last entry in this little green book for a while. Tomorrow we make port, and I'll finally get to hear your voice again.

These pages have been my companion through lonely nights and endless days. Every word written here was written with love, hope, and the absolute certainty that what we have is worth every mile of ocean between us.

Until I hold you again...

Forever yours,
Your sailor
