Our Committees

The daytime club has Hiking, Wine, and Dining Out. So do we. Ours just keep going after the official minutes stop.

💆

Sensual Massage Circle

Chair: Diane · Vice Chair: Also Diane

Third Thursdays. Bring a towel, a signed waiver, and realistic expectations about Gary's grip strength. Essential oils provided; boundaries respected; eye contact optional but encouraged.

🔑

The Swinging Society

Chair: Rotates. Obviously.

Officially a big band dance appreciation group. Unofficially, the bowl by the door at Ted & Marlene's is not for car keys you plan to leave with. Newcomers matched with an experienced mentor couple.

🍄

Recreational Botany Club

Chair: "Ask for Mushroom Steve"

Monthly foraging walks in the foothills where we identify local flora, and then some of us become one with it. Dosage guidance available from Steve, a retired pharmacist who has "seen the fabric of it all."

🥾

Trails & Tabs

Chair: Barefoot Craig

Our microdosing hiking committee. Inspiration Point has never been this inspirational. Moderate 4-mile loop, immoderate appreciation of lichen. Someone sober holds the car keys and the group chat.

🍷

Wine (Feral Division)

Chair: Vacant since the Solvang Incident

The regular Wine Committee swirls and spits. We swirl and commit. Funk Zone tastings that begin with tasting notes and end with someone's sarong in a fountain. Uber budget line item approved 37–0.

🛁

Hot Tub Diplomacy

Chair: Whoever owns the tub

Where the club's real decisions get made. Swimsuits mandatory until 9 PM, at which point the committee enters executive session. Chlorine levels professionally maintained. Trust levels: your problem.

🌊

Butterfly Beach Moonlight Swim

Chair: Prefers anonymity, wears a robe

Full-moon dips off Butterfly Beach. The dress code is described in the bylaws as "traditional." Towel valet provided by the Hospitality Committee, who have seen things and signed NDAs.

🎨

Figure Drawing (Members Model)

Chair: Lorraine, unbothered

Why hire a model when Richard from the Cycling Committee has been dying to volunteer since 2023? Charcoal provided. Flattering angles are the artist's responsibility, not the club's.

🃏

Bridge, But With Stakes

Chair: "The Commissioner"

Grandma's card game, cartel accounting. Buy-ins start at $20 and end at "my timeshare in Cabo." A gentleman named Whit runs the book. We do not ask Whit questions.

📚

Tantric Book Club

Chair: Susan & Susan's husband, technically

We read one book a quarter and practice active listening, sustained eye contact, and breathwork. The book is largely a formality. February's selection has been "in progress" for three years.

🧊

Cold Plunge & Confessions

Chair: Deb, who feels nothing anymore

Sunrise plunges at Leadbetter followed by a sharing circle where the cold does the emotional excavation for you. What's said in the 54-degree water stays in the 54-degree water.

🍲

Polyamory Potluck

Chairs: It's complicated

Everyone brings a dish and a calendar. The sign-up sheet is color-coded and laminated because we learned the hard way. Casserole disputes mediated by the Ethics Subcommittee (also complicated).

🍪

Edibles & Appetizers Pairing

Chair: Chef Marguerite (2.5mg)

Elevated small bites paired with, well, elevation. Everything is labeled, dosed, and beautiful. The 7 PM seating is mellow. Do not sign up for the 5 PM seating thinking you'll drive home at 8.

🪓

Axe Throwing After Wine Tasting

Chair: Our insurance adjuster, reluctantly

Exactly what it sounds like, in exactly that order. The single most popular committee three years running. Zero injuries to date, which our chair describes as "statistically enraging."

🎤

Karaoke Until Someone Cries

Chair: Bill, who starts it

Starts as fun group karaoke, ends when Bill does "Wichita Lineman" and we all think about our first marriages. Kleenex budget is the second-largest line item after the wine budget.

🏓

Pickleball: No-Apology Division

Chair: Sandra "The Kitchen" Delgado

For members asked to leave the regular pickleball group over "tone." We body-bag at the net and we do not say sorry. Winner of the annual grudge match against the daytime club, 4 years running.
