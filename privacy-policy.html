Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-03-14

This privacy policy describes how the "Wild Fruit Messenger" mobile application ("App", "we", "us") handles user information. The App is distributed via the Apple App Store. We are committed to protecting user privacy and minimizing data handling.

Summary

We do not collect, store, or share any personal or device-identifying data from users. The App does not use analytics, advertising identifiers, trackers, or third-party data collection services.

What we do not collect

  • No personal information (name, email, phone number, address).
  • No account information or authentication credentials.
  • No device identifiers or analytics (IDFA, IDFV, advertising identifiers).
  • No location data, contacts, photos, or other private files.

Permissions

The App does not request permissions for the camera, microphone, contacts, location, photos, or other protected resources. If a future feature requires a permission, it will be clearly described in an update and the permission request will be explicit.

Local Data

Any data created and stored by the App is kept locally on the user's device only. We do not transmit local data to our servers or third parties. If the App offers import/export or share features, those actions are initiated by the user and controlled by the device's native sharing mechanisms.

Third-party services

The App does not integrate third-party analytics, advertising SDKs, or trackers. Bundled libraries are limited to those necessary for app functionality; none are used to collect user data.

Data security

Because we do not collect or store user data on our servers, there is minimal risk of centralized data breaches. Users are responsible for device-level security (passcode, encryption, backups) for any local data stored on their device.

Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children. If you believe your child has provided information, contact us and we will take steps to remove such information from any local or cloud storage under our control.

International users

The App does not transfer personal data across borders because no personal data is collected.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in the App or legal requirements. Material changes will be posted with a revised effective date. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance.
